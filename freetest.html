FAQ

Common Questions

Quick answers to the things most people want to know before booking.

How much does it cost to hire the hall?

We offer competitive, affordable pricing tailored to your event type and duration. Call us on 020 8311 7005 for a personalised quote — we are known for being budget-friendly.

Is there parking available?

Yes — we have one dedicated parking space next to the building for Saturday hirers. Free street parking is also available in surrounding roads from 1pm on weekends. We are on a bus route, so parking directly outside is restricted until 6:30pm on Saturdays.

What facilities are included?

Both halls include a kitchenette, baby changing room, and air conditioning. Our large hall comfortably fits up to 120 guests with space for a DJ and dance floor.

How do I get my deposit back?

Our caretaker is on hand to help with clean-up, and deposits are returned promptly after the event — many customers mention receiving theirs back in record time.

How do I get there by public transport?

We are located moments from Abbey Wood Station with excellent bus and train links. A bus stop is right outside the building for added convenience.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Saturday, closing at 9:30pm. Private hall hire is available on Saturdays — book early as spaces fill up fast.

Ready to Host Your Next Event?

Spaces fill up fast — especially for Saturday hire. Give us a call today to check availability and get a personalised quote for your party, meeting, or celebration.
